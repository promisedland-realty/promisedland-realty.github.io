## What Can You Do on Promised Land?

### 🏠 Browse Properties
Explore hundreds of listings across Tamil Nadu — plots, villas, apartments, commercial spaces, warehouses, agricultural land, hotels, and more. Each listing includes photos, location, price, size, and direct WhatsApp contact to the seller.

### 🔍 Search & Filter
Find exactly what you need using the smart search. Filter by district, property type, and budget — from below ₹20 Lakhs to above ₹1 Crore. Click any of the 38 district chips to instantly see properties in that area.

### 📝 List Your Property Free
Selling a property? Post your listing for free in minutes. Fill a simple form with your property details, add a photo, and submit. Our team verifies and publishes your listing within 24 hours. No hidden charges. No commission.

### 🧮 Real Estate Calculators
Promised Land includes four powerful calculators to help you plan your investment:
- **EMI Calculator** — know your monthly home loan payment before you buy
- **Stamp Duty Calculator** — estimate registration costs in Tamil Nadu
- **Rental Yield Calculator** — find out if a property is a good investment
- **Appreciation Calculator** — see how much your property could grow in value

### ⚠️ Scam Protection
We take buyer safety seriously. Promised Land displays a clear fraud warning on every page — reminding users to never pay in advance, always verify documents, and contact us directly to verify any agent claiming to represent us.
